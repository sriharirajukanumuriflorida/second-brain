## Summary
- Extends neural networks from simple ANN structures into deeper multi-layer architectures.
- Introduces forward propagation as a key mechanism for passing information through the network.

## Key Ideas
- Deep neural networks stack multiple hidden layers to learn more complex representations.
- Forward propagation computes outputs layer by layer from inputs to predictions.
- Depth increases learning capacity, but also raises training and optimization challenges.
