## Summary
- Introduces recurrent neural networks for sequence-based data such as text and time-dependent inputs.
- Extends the course from image-focused models into sequential learning problems.

## Key Ideas
- RNNs model temporal or ordered dependencies across sequences.
- LSTMs address some of the limitations of basic RNNs for longer dependencies.
- Sequence modeling opens applications in NLP, time series, and video-related tasks.

## Important Details
- This lesson shifts the course from spatial data processing toward sequential and temporal modeling.
- The notebooks cover text classification with RNNs and LSTMs, plus a hybrid video classification example.
