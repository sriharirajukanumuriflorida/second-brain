## Summary
- Introduces transformers as a modern architecture for natural language processing tasks.
- Connects transformer-based representations to practical NLP workflows such as BERT-based text classification.

## Key Ideas
- Transformers improve sequence modeling by relying on attention rather than recurrence alone.
- BERT is a major pretrained transformer model for language understanding tasks.
- Pretrained language models can be adapted effectively to downstream NLP applications.

## Important Details
- This lesson builds on the sequence-modeling foundation from RNNs but moves to more powerful transformer-based methods.
- The notebooks cover BERT introduction and text classification implementation.
