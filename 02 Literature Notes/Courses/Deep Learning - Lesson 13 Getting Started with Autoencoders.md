## Summary
- Introduces autoencoders as neural networks designed to learn compact internal representations.
- Connects encoding, decoding, and reconstruction to practical unsupervised learning workflows.

## Key Ideas
- Autoencoders compress input data into a latent representation and reconstruct it.
- They are useful for representation learning, dimensionality reduction, and anomaly-related tasks.
- Reconstruction quality helps reveal how well the model captures the underlying data structure.

## Important Details
- This lesson closes the course by introducing a different deep learning objective than classification or sequence prediction.
