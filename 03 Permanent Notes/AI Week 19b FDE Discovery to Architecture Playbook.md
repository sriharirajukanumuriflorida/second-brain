# AI Week 19b FDE Discovery to Architecture Playbook

FDE workflow from vague ask to defensible AI architecture:

1. Restate the business ask in the customer's language.
2. Discover users, workflow, pain, source systems, data boundaries, compliance needs, launch blockers, and success metrics.
3. Draw current state before future state; identify where AI assists and where humans remain accountable.
4. Choose architecture components only after constraints are visible: identity, retrieval, model, prompt registry, guardrails, cache, audit, observability, and human review.
5. Write ADRs with context, decision, consequences, and rejected alternatives.
6. Do capacity math early enough to kill fantasy designs.
7. Name failure modes and map each to a mitigation, owner, metric, and runbook.
8. Hand off artifacts that survive outside the meeting: one-pager, ADRs, risk register, eval plan, backlog, and acceptance criteria.

> One-liner: **the FDE job is not to draw boxes; it is to make the customer's next engineering decision safer.**
