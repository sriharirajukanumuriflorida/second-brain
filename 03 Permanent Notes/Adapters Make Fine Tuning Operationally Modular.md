# Adapters Make Fine Tuning Operationally Modular

Adapters turn fine-tuning into small deployable artifacts that can be trained, evaluated, rolled back, merged, or hot-swapped while a base model stays fixed.

> One-liner: **PEFT saves memory, but its production gift is modularity.**
