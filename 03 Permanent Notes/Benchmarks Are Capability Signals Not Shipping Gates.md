# Benchmarks Are Capability Signals Not Shipping Gates

Benchmark Literacy matters because it turns a vague quality concern into inspectable evidence. The durable pattern is: define cases, score them with a documented metric or rubric, inspect slices, compare to a baseline, and make a decision rule explicit.

> One-liner: **measure only what you are willing to act on.**
