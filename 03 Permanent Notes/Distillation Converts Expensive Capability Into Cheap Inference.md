# Distillation Converts Expensive Capability Into Cheap Inference

A teacher model can label data, provide soft distributions, or generate rationales so a smaller student learns the behavior. The goal is not maximum benchmark score; it is enough quality at much lower latency and cost.

> One-liner: **train expensive, serve cheap when the task allows.**
