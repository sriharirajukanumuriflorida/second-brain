# FDE Week 23a Discovery Question Bank

Use this before every customer discovery meeting.

## Executive sponsor, 30 minutes
- What business metric must move for this to be considered worth funding? **Why it matters:** anchors the MVP to business value rather than novelty.
- Why now, and what happens if nothing changes this quarter? **Why it matters:** reveals urgency, deadlines, and political stakes.
- Who owns budget, go-live approval, and operational adoption? **Why it matters:** separates interested executives from accountable sponsors.
- What must not break during the pilot? **Why it matters:** exposes non-negotiables and risk boundaries.

## Domain SME, 60 minutes
- Walk through the workflow from trigger to final decision, including exceptions. **Why it matters:** real workflows live in exceptions.
- Which cases are easy, hard, rare, or dangerous? **Why it matters:** eval sets need representative and high-risk slices.
- What evidence makes an answer trustworthy? **Why it matters:** defines citations, explanations, and acceptance criteria.
- What would a junior person get wrong? **Why it matters:** captures tacit expertise the system must surface or avoid.

## End user, 45 minutes
- When do you lose time today? **Why it matters:** finds adoption-driving pain.
- What workaround do you use that leadership may not know about? **Why it matters:** shadow workflows reveal product requirements.
- When would you distrust the assistant? **Why it matters:** trust thresholds shape UX, refusal, and review.
- What output would save you one click, one search, or one escalation? **Why it matters:** small workflow wins compound.

## Data and security, 30 minutes
- Where is the data, who owns it, and what is the access path? **Why it matters:** data readiness often gates the MVP.
- Which fields are PHI, PII, regulated, retained, or prohibited from logs? **Why it matters:** defines the data boundary.
- What audit evidence must exist after every recommendation? **Why it matters:** compliance needs traceability.
- What provider, region, and retention constraints apply? **Why it matters:** architecture choices may be predetermined.

## Existing engineering, 30 minutes
- Which integration surfaces are available now versus aspirational? **Why it matters:** prevents MVPs from depending on nonexistent APIs.
- Who can deploy, operate, and respond to incidents? **Why it matters:** handoff starts in discovery.
- What systems are brittle, frozen, or politically sensitive? **Why it matters:** protects the FDE from accidental blast radius.
- What observability and release process already exists? **Why it matters:** the MVP should fit the customer's operating model.

> One-liner: **ask until the vague AI ask becomes a user, workflow, data path, metric, constraint, risk, and non-negotiable.**
