# FDE Week 23a Executive Communication Playbook

Reusable communication patterns for FDE engagements.

## BLUF
Start with the answer: recommendation, expected business impact, primary risk, and decision needed. If an executive reads only the first four lines, they should know what to do.

## Pyramid Principle
Structure the one-pager as **Situation → Complication → Question → Answer**. Keep supporting analysis below the answer, not before it.

## Three-options rule
Present exactly three meaningful options:
1. **Aggressive**: broader scope, faster business visibility, higher delivery or AI-quality risk.
2. **Recommended**: best risk-adjusted path inside the timebox.
3. **Safe**: narrower scope, more certainty, lower visible ambition.

Never present one option unless the decision is already made; never present five or more unless the executive asked for an appendix.

## Demo three-act arc
- **Setup:** user's real-world problem and baseline metric.
- **Confrontation:** current pain, ambiguity, and the assistant's grounded response.
- **Resolution:** measured outcome, eval score, cost, limitation, and next decision.

## One-pager template
- BLUF recommendation and ask.
- Situation, Complication, Question, Answer.
- MVP scope: Must, Should, Won't.
- Metrics: business metric, eval metric, latency, cost, audit.
- Risks: top three with owner and mitigation.
- Decision: approve scope, unblock data, assign owner, or accept tradeoff.

> One-liner: **a demo without eval numbers is a magic trick; an executive summary without a decision ask is a status update.**
