# FDE Week 23b Enterprise AI Engagement Playbook

Reusable six-month enterprise AI engagement rhythm:

1. **Weeks 1-2 discovery**: stakeholder map; executive, SME, end-user, data/security, engineering, and procurement interviews; DiscoveryBrief; JTBD; SMART metrics; pre-mortem; constraints; decision log.
2. **Weeks 2-3 scope approval**: translate epics/PBIs with functional plus eval acceptance criteria; score 10-15 feature candidates with RICE-for-AI; run MoSCoW workshop; present three options; record signed Won't list.
3. **Weeks 3-6 architecture and backlog**: architecture one-pager, ADRs, capacity/cost model, eval plan, security assumptions, release tuple, sprint backlog.
4. **Weeks 6-16 build and pilot hardening**: weekly steering, biweekly SME eval curation, demo clips with eval numbers, risk register updates, cost and refusal/hallucination tuning.
5. **Weeks 16-22 SRB and go-live readiness**: model-risk submission, red-team evidence, 25-item checklist, runbooks, rollback drills, SLO contract.
6. **Weeks 22-24 go-live and handoff**: hypercare, engagement wrap, owner map, runbook links, v2 roadmap, adoption dashboard, and post-FDE operating cadence.

Cadences: weekly 30m exec steering; biweekly 60m SME working session; monthly 45m risk committee; budget memo on variance; same-day sponsor note on material risk tradeoffs.

> One-liner: **an FDE engagement plan is a trust cadence: discover, decide, build, prove, hand off.**
