# FDE Week 23b Hard Conversation Templates for AI Engagements

Use these three-paragraph openers when the engagement needs clarity.

- **Scope cut**: BLUF: to preserve the signed outcome and date, I recommend moving X to v2. Why: X depends on missing data/eval/security evidence and would put Y at risk. Path: we will collect evidence during v1 and revisit at trigger Z.
- **Cost overrun**: BLUF: forecast changed by N%, but we have a mitigation and decision point. Facts: driver is tokens/PTU/logging/users, not unexplained spend. Recommendation: choose Standard/PTU/cache/sampling path with revisit threshold.
- **Refusal tuning**: BLUF: the assistant refused a legitimate question; we reproduced it and propose threshold change T. Tradeoff: valid-answer rate improves by A while hallucination changes by B. Control: add case to golden set and monitor next sprint.
- **Hallucination incident**: BLUF: an unsupported answer occurred and is contained. Impact: users/data affected, decision made or not made, audit evidence preserved. Prevention: rollback/fix, golden-set addition, owner, date.
- **Provider outage**: BLUF: provider degradation is affecting assistant availability/latency. Mitigation: fallback/manual/cached mode active. Update: next checkpoint and business impact.
- **SRB re-plan**: BLUF: SRB requires additional evidence before go-live; recommendation is to re-scope final weeks. Change: defer one nice-to-have, add red-team/checklist closure. Impact: core business benefit remains intact; production risk is reduced.
- **Adoption pushback**: BLUF: adoption risk is real and addressable. Evidence: users distrust X or workflow Y. Plan: SME champions, training, feedback loop, UI/citation fix.
- **Handoff resistance**: BLUF: the system cannot be FDE-owned after go-live. Owner map: platform/product/risk/SME responsibilities. Support: hypercare window and runbook links.

> One-liner: **hard conversations land when they name the change, quantify the tradeoff, and recommend the next decision.**
