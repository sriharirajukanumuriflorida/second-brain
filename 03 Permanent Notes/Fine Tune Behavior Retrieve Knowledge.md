# Fine Tune Behavior Retrieve Knowledge

Use fine-tuning for stable behavior and retrieval for changing knowledge. SFT is the right lever for repeated format, tone, workflow, or tool-use failures; RAG is the right lever for fresh documents, provenance, tenant-specific records, and auditability.

> One-liner: **behavior in weights, knowledge in context.**
