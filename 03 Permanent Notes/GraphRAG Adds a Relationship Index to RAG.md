# GraphRAG Adds a Relationship Index to RAG

GraphRAG builds a second index over a corpus: entities, relations, communities, and summaries with provenance back to source chunks. This lets retrieval follow relationships and aggregate themes, not just find semantically similar passages.

It is most useful when answers depend on connections across documents: who owns what, what depends on what, what risks cluster together, or what themes dominate a corpus.

> One-liner: **vectors find similar text; graphs find connected facts.**
