# GraphRAG Needs Provenance or It Becomes Uncited Claims

Every GraphRAG node, edge, and community summary should retain provenance to source chunks. Without provenance, the graph becomes another ungrounded knowledge source: fluent answers can cite neither the original text nor the extraction that produced the relation.

Provenance turns graph traversal into auditable evidence.

> One-liner: **a graph edge without provenance is just a claim.**
