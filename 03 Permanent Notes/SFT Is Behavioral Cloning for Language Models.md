# SFT Is Behavioral Cloning for Language Models

Production SFT teaches a model to imitate demonstrations: prompts plus preferred responses. The examples define the response policy — structure, style, refusal behavior, and task procedure. Because SFT copies behavior, bad labels become bad habits.

> One-liner: **SFT copies the behavior in your examples, so make the examples the product you want.**
