# Tables Are Two Dimensional Evidence Not Paragraphs

Tables encode relationships across rows, columns, headers, units, and captions. Flattening a table into arbitrary text loses the very structure a question may depend on. For LLM use, Markdown tables often preserve readability; for computation, JSON or CSV preserves exact cells. Always attach table id, page, section, and source metadata.

> One-liner: **serialize tables as structure, not as scrambled prose**.
